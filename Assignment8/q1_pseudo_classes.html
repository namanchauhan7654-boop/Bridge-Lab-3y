Interactive examples of :hover, :focus, :active, and :nth-child() aligned in one row.

1. :hover State

Hover Over Link

Triggers when the pointer rests over the link, shifting its background and text color smoothly.

2. :focus State

Activates when an element receives keyboard or mouse focus, highlighting the input border.

3. :active State

Applies at the exact moment a user clicks down on the button, darkening its fill.
