CSS Gradients & Fixed Parallax Layout

1. Linear Gradient Background

This section uses a multi-stop diagonal linear-gradient(135deg, #1e3a8a, #3b82f6, #06b6d4).

Linear gradients transition colors smoothly along a straight line defined by an angle or direction keyword.

2. Radial Gradient & Fixed Attachment

This section applies radial-gradient(circle at center, #f43f5e, #881337, #1e1b4b) combined with background-attachment: fixed;.

As you scroll past this section, the gradient remains locked to the viewport, creating a smooth parallax-like depth effect.
